Avatar

An avatar shows a person's initials. Place it beside their name so the person can be identified without relying on the initials or colour. The macro hides the avatar from assistive technology with aria-hidden="true".

Sizes

Three sizes are available: 24px by default, md at 32px, and lg at 48px.

Colour pool

Choose from six colours: purple, orange, green, red, teal, and pink. Assign colours consistently so that a person keeps the same colour across pages. Your application decides how to make that assignment, for example by hashing a stable user identifier.
