Article card

Use an article card to show one submission in a list. It includes the article title, identifying details, status, and available actions. Alert pills provide links to related problems or updates.

Severity variants

Add article-card-critical, article-card-warning, or article-card-info for the coloured left border. Omit all three for the neutral state.

Notes

  • Use text to explain the status represented by the coloured border, such as the alert pills below the card.
  • Your application determines the warning thresholds and any title truncation. The examples treat over 60 days as critical and over 30 days as a warning, and set the matching article-card-days-critical or article-card-days-warning class on the days indicator.
  • Use btn-small for actions within the card.
