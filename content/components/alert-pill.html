Alert pill

An alert pill links to a problem or update associated with an article. Place alert pills below the article's main details, at the bottom of an article card.

Variants

Choose the critical, warning, or information variant to match the message: alert-pill-critical, alert-pill-warning, or alert-pill-info. Use alert-pill-more to show the number of additional alerts.

Notes

  • Pills with a destination render as links.
  • An alert-pill-more pill is a text count of the remaining alerts. It does not expand or collapse anything.
  • Your application decides when an alert is critical or a warning. The 60-day and 30-day thresholds used in the article card examples are an application convention; the component does not calculate them.
