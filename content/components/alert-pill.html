Alert pill

A small inline status link used at the bottom of an article card to surface alerts.

Variants

Four variants compose: alert-pill-critical, alert-pill-warning, alert-pill-info, alert-pill-more (for the “+N more” collapse).

Notes

  • Pills with a destination render as links; the alert-pill-more collapse is plain text.
  • Severity thresholds match the article card days indicator: over 60 days critical, over 30 days warning.
