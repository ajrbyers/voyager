Panel

Use a panel to show the outcome of an action on a completion page, such as Decision sent, Article accepted, or Submission received.

Default (success)

Variants

Notes

  • The macro renders the title as an <h1>, so use one panel per page and do not add a second <h1>.
  • Put a reference number in <strong> on its own line so users can read it back or quote it.
  • Place relevant next actions below the panel, such as View article or Return to submissions. A back link can accompany them.
