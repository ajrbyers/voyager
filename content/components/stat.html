Stat

A stat displays a count and its label, such as the number of overdue reviews. Use it in a dashboard summary or a queue card.

Variants

Variants: stat-critical (red), stat-success (green), stat-warning (orange).

Notes

  • Show the full count, such as 14,000 rather than 14k.
