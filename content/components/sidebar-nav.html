Sidebar nav

A left rail of grouped navigation links. Use it for section-level navigation within a journal, alongside the app header at the top of the page.

Grouped sections

Each section has an <h2> label and a <ul> of links. Below 900px the sections collapse behind the Menu button in the journal row; the shared disclosure driver in folder-list.js toggles them via data-disclosure-toggle, aria-controls and aria-expanded.
