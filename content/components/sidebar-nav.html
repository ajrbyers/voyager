Sidebar nav

Use the sidebar for navigation within a journal. Group related pages under headings such as Workflow and Content. Place it below the app header.

Grouped sections

Each section has an <h2> label and a <ul> of links.

At widths of 900px or less, users can expand the navigation with Menu in the journal row. The disclosure script updates the expanded state.

The shared disclosure driver in folder-list.js toggles the sections through data-disclosure-toggle, aria-controls, and aria-expanded.
