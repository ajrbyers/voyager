Modal

Dialog with a backdrop, header, body, and footer. Used for confirmations and short focused actions. The component driver (modal.js) responds to a control with data-modal-open="<backdrop-id>" opens the modal; anything inside carrying data-modal-close closes it, as do Esc and a click on the backdrop. Focus is trapped while open and returned to the opener on close.

Confirm a destructive action

Notes

  • Always set aria-modal="true" and aria-labelledby on the dialog.
  • The opening control should receive focus back when the modal closes.
  • Reserve modals for short focused tasks. For longer flows (a decision form, a settings page), navigate to a full page.
  • Don't nest modals.
