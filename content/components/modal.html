Modal

Use a modal for a confirmation or a short task that can be completed without leaving the page. Use a separate page for longer forms.

Set data-modal-open to the backdrop's ID on the opening control. Controls with data-modal-close close the dialog. Users can also close it with Esc or by selecting the backdrop. The driver (modal.js) is single-open: opening a modal while another is open dismisses the first.

Focus stays within the dialog while it is open, and everything outside it is made inert, so background content cannot be reached by keyboard or assistive technology. When the dialog closes, focus returns to the control that opened it, provided that control is still visible.

Select Withdraw reviewer… to open the example dialog.

Notes

  • Set aria-modal="true" and aria-labelledby on the dialog. The macro does this for you.
  • Reserve modals for short tasks. Use a full page for a longer flow, such as a decision form or a settings page.
  • Do not open a modal from inside another modal. The driver dismisses the first one rather than stacking them.
