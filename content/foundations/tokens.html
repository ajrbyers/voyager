Tokens

Design tokens define Voyager's colours, spacing, and shadows. Raw tokens hold values, and semantic tokens assign them to interface roles.

Borders

--colour-border-muted#d8dee4
--colour-border#d0d7de
--colour-border-strong#6e7781
--colour-border-component#b1b4b6

Choose border colours according to their purpose. A subtle divider and a form-control boundary have different contrast requirements. Check the border against the background where it appears: WCAG 1.4.11 requires ≥3:1 contrast for a border that distinguishes a control from its background.

Shadow

Most Voyager components have square corners, and there are no radius tokens. Rounded shapes - avatars, dots, radio buttons, and step markers - set border-radius:50% directly. Overlay shadows distinguish menus and dialogs from the content behind them. Buttons use a 2px bottom shadow.

  • --shadow-overlay 0 4px 12px rgba(11,12,12,0.16) - dropdowns and popovers
  • --shadow-modal 0 16px 32px rgba(11,12,12,0.2) - modal dialogs
